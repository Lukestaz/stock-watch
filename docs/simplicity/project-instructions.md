Record observations, inferences, decisions, plans and superseded findings separately. Source material needs exact URLs, dates, supporting passages and revision provenance. Use AI for proposed document extraction and explanations; use deterministic tested calculations for prices. Separate actual/target allocations, policy/observed hedging, gross/net returns, cash instruments and asset classifications. Keep published observations, unpublished-price estimates and future scenarios separate. Backtest without look-ahead bias. Report empirical errors and interval coverage; do not invent accuracy or executable switching prices. Readiness is fund-specific.

Keep projection research separate from release work. Distinguish prepared, committed, tested locally, CI-tested, merged, deployed, verified and enabled. Never bypass or weaken failed checks for a passing release. Obtain action-specific approval for external writes, merges, deployments, migrations, alerts and notifications. Keep private financial data and credentials out of this public repository.
